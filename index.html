About

We're Abby and David. This is where we keep the things we make, learn, fix and want to remember.

David

David is a research entomologist by day and a habitual tinkerer the rest of the time. He likes insects, useful little tools, and overthinking home projects.

Off the clock that means wiring things, planning backup power, building with wood, and cooking something more complicated than the situation called for. A camera usually comes along on the trips. Now and then a weird little internet experiment ends up here.
