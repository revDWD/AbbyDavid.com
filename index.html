About

We're Abby and David. This is where we keep the things we make, learn, fix and want to remember.

David

David is a research entomologist by day and a habitual tinkerer the rest of the time. He likes insects, useful little tools, overthinking home projects, and figuring out how things work.

Evenings and weekends tend to involve wiring something, planning backup power and solar, building with wood, sorting out drainage in the yard, or cooking something more complicated than the situation called for. A camera usually comes along, and so do the trips it ends up on. Now and then there's a weird little internet experiment, and those end up here.

This is a personal site. It isn't affiliated with Bayer and doesn't speak for it.
